Explore the research

BIGJN examines the rules, market practices, and consumer impacts that shape gambling systems. Public summaries are available now; searchable regulatory datasets and interactive dashboards are planned.

Research priorities

  • Reviewing regulation, licensing, consumer protections, and harm-reduction measures.
  • Making methods, sources, and the limits of available evidence clear.
  • Connecting research findings to practical policy questions.
