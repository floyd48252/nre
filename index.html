Who we are

Betting, I-gaming & Gambling Justice Network

We believe the global betting, i-gaming, and gambling industries hold immense potential for fostering economic growth and societal well-being. However, to achieve this, fairness, transparency, and accountability must be at the core of these industries.

Our mission is to combat exploitative practices, advocate for responsible gambling, and promote fair distribution of revenues to support societal development. Through research, advocacy, and collaboration with stakeholders, we aim to create an industry that prioritizes ethics, transparency, and the protection of vulnerable individuals.
